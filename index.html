Our Story

Where Coffee Was Born

Yemen is the birthplace of coffee as the world knows it. For centuries, the ancient port city of Mocha gave the world its first taste of this extraordinary beverage — traded along spice routes, brewed in the courtyards of old stone cities, and shared as an act of welcome.

At Sahara Coffee Co., we carry that legacy to Milford, Connecticut. Every cup we serve is an invitation — to slow down, to connect, and to taste history. From our earthy, spiced qishr to our hand-brewed single-origin bun, our coffee is made with intention and served with warmth.
